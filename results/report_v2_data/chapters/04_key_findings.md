## Key Findings

### The Landscape

![Scatter plot: d/acc alignment vs technology impact](scatter_plot.png)

*29 of 39 entities plotted; 10 excluded due to missing technology impact scores.*

The scatter plot below maps all 39 entities by d/acc alignment and technology impact. Each entity was scored on three dimensions: whether it qualifies as a hyper-entity, its potential technology impact (0–70), and its alignment with d/acc values (0–20, converted to percentages in the plot). The d/acc framework evaluates whether a technology distributes power rather than concentrating it, strengthens defense over offense, and enables broad cooperation. Technology impact captures scale, feasibility, and transformative potential. Full scoring methodology is in the appendix.

The Existential Hope corpus skews toward well-aligned technologies: most entities sit in the 65–90% d/acc range, reflecting the community's orientation rather than the broader technology landscape. High alignment and high impact don't reliably coincide, though. Several of the strongest values-alignment scores are in governance and coordination, where the transformation is social and institutional rather than technical, and where impact is harder to quantify. The entities that combine both occupy a relatively sparse corner of the plot. Most are early: 20 at early demonstrations, 11 at foundational research, 6 at scaling challenges, and only 2 near deployment. The window for shaping these systems is still open.

### Most Underattended

The entities combining high d/acc alignment with high technology impact (the sparse upper-right corner of the plot) and receiving the least funding and visibility relative to those scores are:

- **Automated Scientific Publishing for Machine Consumers** (d/acc: 90%, Tech: 86%): infrastructure for AI-native knowledge sharing, enabling scientific progress to compound at machine speed
- **LexCommons** (d/acc: 90%, Tech: 81%): open-source legal infrastructure, building on LexDAO's early work toward accessible governance tooling
- **Gevulot (Privacy Infrastructure)** (d/acc: 90%): cryptographic privacy infrastructure; technology impact not yet assessed
- **Epistemic Stack** (d/acc: 85%, Tech: 63%): tools for establishing shared understanding and collaborative reasoning across communities
- **Universal AI Learning UnCommons (UALU)** (d/acc: 85%, Tech: 63%): open educational infrastructure for the AI era
- **Conflict De-escalation Protocol** (d/acc: 80%): formal frameworks for reducing interstate and institutional conflict
- **Chemputing (Chemical Computing)** (d/acc: 65%, Tech: 63%): molecular-scale computing with low energy footprint and broad applicability
